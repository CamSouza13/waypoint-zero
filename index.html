Services

What we build

Most jobs use one of four methods. Some use two, like a printed prototype followed by a stainless final part.

3D-printed parts

Brackets, bezels, clips, covers, and replacement plastic parts.

  • MaterialsASA, PETG, carbon-fiber nylon
  • Best forComplex shapes, small runs
  • Typical lead time1 to 2 weeks

Canvas and upholstery

Covers, biminis, enclosures, screens, and cushions patterned on the boat.

  • MaterialsSolution-dyed acrylic, marine vinyl
  • Best forSun, rain, and UV protection
  • Typical lead time2 to 6 weeks

Metal and HDPE fabrication

Mounts, backing plates, rails, barriers, and brackets built to our drawings.

  • Materials316 stainless, aluminum, marine HDPE
  • Best forLoad-bearing and exposed parts
  • Typical lead time2 to 4 weeks

Replacement parts

Send the broken part or photos with measurements. We model it, reinforce it, and make a new one.

  • InputsBroken part, photos, or template
  • Best forDiscontinued and one-off parts
  • Typical lead time1 to 3 weeks

Fabrication runs in our shop or with our marine fabrication partners, always to our drawings.

Systems and repair

Electrical, solar, and retrofit design

The parts are only half of it. We also design the systems they serve: wiring, charging, solar, and battery banks. We repair what's already on the boat too.

EXAMPLE SINGLE-LINE DIAGRAMDWG WZ-E-001

Electrical system design

DC and AC systems drawn for your boat, from a single new circuit to a full rewire.

  • Wiring diagrams and panel layouts
  • Load calculations, wire and fuse sizing
  • Drawn to ABYC E-11

Solar and charging

Arrays sized to what you actually use, with mounts designed for your rails, arch, or bimini.

  • Energy budget and array sizing
  • Controller, alternator, and shore charging
  • Panel mount drawings and fabrication

Lithium battery upgrades

Lead-acid to LiFePO4 conversions planned around your charging sources and loads.

  • Bank sizing and BMS selection
  • Alternator and charger protection
  • Planned to ABYC E-13

Retrofit design

Refits for older boats. New electronics, helm and panel layouts, and hardware that fits the boat you have.

  • Helm and electronics layouts
  • Custom panels, pods, and mounts in CAD
  • Parts list and install sequence

Part repair and replication

Broken or discontinued parts modeled, reinforced, and made again. Often stronger than the original.

  • Mail in the part or send photos
  • CAD model kept on file for reorders
  • 3D print, stainless, or HDPE

Canvas repair

Restitching, zippers, windows, and chafe patches for biminis, dodgers, covers, and cushions.

  • UV thread restitching
  • Zipper and window replacement
  • Chafe patches at wear points

Each project starts with a survey at the boat or from your photos and measurements. You get drawings, a parts list, and a quote. We install it ourselves or through a certified marine electrician.

Ordering

Shipping, returns, and warranty

The short version of how orders work. Details come with your order confirmation.

Shipping and install

We make store parts to order and ship them within the lead time listed on each part. In San Diego County we can install them for you.

Returns

You can return unused store parts within 30 days of delivery. Custom parts made for one boat can't be returned, so we test fit them before the final build.

Warranty

Every part carries a one-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. If a part fails in normal use, we repair or replace it.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

If yours isn't here, add it to your quote request.

Do you only work in San Diego?

We measure and install on site in San Diego County. For boats elsewhere, we work from your photos and templates and ship the part with installation instructions.

What if a custom part doesn't fit?

We test fit a prototype on the boat before making the final part. Changes happen at that stage.

My boat isn't listed on a store part. Will it fit?

Send us the make, model, and a photo of the area. Many parts fit more boats than we list, and we can adjust the file if yours is different.

Can you copy a part that broke?

Yes. Send the pieces or photos with measurements. We rebuild it in CAD and usually strengthen the area that failed.

Do you work with other shops?

Yes. Boatyards, riggers, canvas shops, and yacht managers send us design and fabrication work under trade terms.

How do you price custom work?

We quote each custom job after reviewing the details, with design, prototype, and production listed separately. Store parts have a listed price.
